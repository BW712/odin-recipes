Marinated Flank Steak

Home

Description

A great flank steak marinade like this one is important if you want a tender, juicy, flavorful steak. This wonderful quick and easy recipe also works great when the steak is sliced and used for fajitas.

Ingredients

Marinade:

  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 1/3 cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper

Steak:

  • 1(1/2-pound)flank steak

Steps:

  1. Step 1: Gather all ingredients.
  2. Step 2: Whisk together oil, vinegar, lemon juice, Worcestershire sauce, Dijon mustard, garlic and pepper. Thoroughly combined.
  3. Step 3: Pour marinade over the steak, turn several times to coat thoroughly with marinade. Cover and refrigerate for 2 to 6 hours.
  4. Step 4: When ready to cook, preheat the grill for medium-high heat and lightly oil the grate.
  5. Step 5: Remove steak from the marinade and shake off excess.
  6. Step 6: Cook Steak on the preheated gull for about 5 minutes per side, ot to desired doneness.
  7. Step 7: Remove from the grill and let rest for 5 minuted before slicing and serving.
  8. Step 8: Serve hot and enjoy!
